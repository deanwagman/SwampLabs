Lexidex support

How can we help?

Find compatibility information and answers to common questions, or contact Swamp Labs for help with Lexidex.

Contact support

Email support@swamp-labs.com. Please include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send private study content unless it is necessary to explain the issue.

Apple Intelligence compatibility

AI-assisted learning-path and card generation requires a device and operating system version that support Apple Intelligence. Apple Intelligence must be enabled and its on-device model must have finished downloading.

If generation is unavailable

  1. Open the Settings app on your device.
  2. Open Apple Intelligence & Siri.
  3. Confirm Apple Intelligence is enabled and ready.
  4. Return to Lexidex and try again.

You can always use Build manually to create learning paths, chapters, and study cards without AI generation.

Study reminders

Lexidex schedules reminders locally on your device. If reminders do not appear, confirm that notifications are enabled for Lexidex in the Settings app and that a reminder time is selected inside Lexidex.

Data and backups

This version of Lexidex stores your learning data locally. It does not provide cloud backup, account recovery, or device-to-device sync. Deleting the app deletes its locally stored database.

Privacy

Lexidex does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, or a Lexidex server that collects your learning activity. Read the complete Lexidex Privacy Policy.
