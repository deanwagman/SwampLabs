Lexidex Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026. Lexidex is designed to keep your learning activity on your device.

Data storage

Your profile, subjects, generated decks, cards, study history, and preferences are stored locally in the Lexidex app database on your device. Lexidex does not provide an account or cloud backup service.

Deleting the app deletes its locally stored data. Removing the app does not delete any local notifications that iOS may have already delivered.

Generation

When you generate a learning path, Lexidex uses Apple Intelligence on your device. Your subject and generated content are not sent to a Lexidex server. Apple’s handling of Apple Intelligence is governed by Apple’s own policies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Lexidex schedules study reminders locally on your device. Lexidex does not use a remote push-notification service.

Data collection and sharing

This version of Lexidex does not include accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, or a Lexidex server that collects your learning data. Lexidex does not sell or share your personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@swamp-labs.com.
